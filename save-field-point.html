Read the steps ↓

From a map location to a field record.

  1. Choose a writable layer. Open Layers and select a normal visible layer. A hidden, locked, trashed or web-service layer cannot receive the pin.
  2. Add and name the point. Tap Add Pin, name the point and save it.
  3. Add your note. Open the saved point. Add the observation in Information and finish editing the card.
  4. Check the saved details. Close the card and reopen the point to check its name and note. Use Show to bring it back onto the map.

Point and note questions

Why can’t I add a point?

Check the active layer. Choose a normal visible writable layer rather than a locked, trashed or web-service layer.

Does the video show the Android interface?

The screenshots show iPhone. Use the Android guide for its controls and available options.

MapNero Practice Island

An original fictional GeoPDF for practising map import.
WGS84 / EPSG:4326. One page. Map viewport: [60,190,660,710] PDF points.
Longitude 0.0000 to 0.0200 E; latitude 0.0000 to 0.0200 N.
A: 0.0060 N, 0.0044 E
B: 0.0084 N, 0.0082 E
C: 0.0138 N, 0.0144 E

The island, contours and gold path are fictional. No actual terrain, elevation or recorded GPS track is represented. Do not use this map for navigation. Your phone location will usually be outside this practice area.

Import: https://www.mapnero.com/geopdf-maps.html#practice-map

Import a map

No map file yet? Download the small practice Geo PDF from Map Nero. This island is fictional. On iPhone or iPad, open Import Map, then Select from Files. Choose your PDF. On Android, choose Add Map, then Import PDF Map. Open the map and check its reference points. Get the free sample at Map Nero dot com.

Check offline access

Before leaving coverage, finish importing your map. Keep the original file. If you need a basemap region, download that separately. Check the area and zoom levels. Turn on airplane mode and reopen the map. Check the area and detail you need, before the trip. Map Nero dot com has the guides. Access varies by plan.

Check map position

Your map opened. Is it in the right place? Compare several known landmarks across the map. In MapNero, Location Outside can mean your phone is outside the map's bounds. That message alone does not prove the file is unreferenced. Check the file format and coordinate system before changing settings. Explore MapNero and the field guides at mapnero dot com.

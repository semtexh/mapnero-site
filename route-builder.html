Build and check your route

  1. Choose the destination layer. Select a visible, unlocked layer, then open More → Route Builder.
  2. Place the first point. Pan the map until the centre crosshair is over the starting point. Tap Add Waypoint.
  3. Extend and review. Move the crosshair and add at least one more point. Check the waypoint list and total distance. Use a row’s minus button to remove a mistaken point.
  4. Save to the layer. With at least two points, use the green Save to Layer button. Closing with X discards an unsaved draft.

Understand what the line represents

Route Builder connects your chosen points directly. It does not automatically find roads or a safe walking route. Check access and terrain separately before travelling.

A drawn route is a plan, not a recorded trip with real travel times or speeds. The 356 m value in this video belongs to the example screen; it is not an independently surveyed ground distance.

Route planning questions

Do I need to stand at the starting point?

No. Move the map to put the crosshair over the position you want. Your GPS position does not need to be there.

What does the 3D button do?

It opens a terrain-elevation preview. Elevation lookup can need network access and permission to send coordinates to an external service. Declining the lookup does not prevent drawing a route.

MAPNERO AT A GLANCE

Your maps, ready for fieldwork.

MapNero is a GIS mapping app for iPhone, iPad, Android and macOS. Its interface supports 20 languages. Features, file support and limits depend on your platform and plan.

Start with your own map

Import a supported local map, check its position and test it offline before leaving coverage. GeoTIFF support differs by platform.

GeoTIFF on mobile →

Record a route

Prepare the map separately, check location permissions and test a saved GPS track. Background recording depends on device settings.

Offline GPS recording →

Move field data

Use supported exchange formats and check coordinates, records and fields on both sides. Keep the original QGIS files until the transfer is verified.

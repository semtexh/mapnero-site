Purchases and subscriptions

Manage your subscription

Use the store account that billed you to manage or cancel renewal. Follow the App Store instructions or the Google Play instructions. Your store shows when your current access ends.

Restore paid access

Open the upgrade screen in MapNero and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account or Google Account you bought from. If you linked your purchase to a MapNero account, sign in to that account first.

If paid access is still missing, email support with your device model, MapNero version and the store you bought from. Keep your password and payment card details private.

Request a refund

Follow the Apple refund instructions or Google Play’s refund process. If you need help with a Google Play purchase, email MapNero support.
